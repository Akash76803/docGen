# Phase 6.1.3A — Starter Templates & Floral Asset Pack

Adds a curated default template gallery and embedded decorative vector library to the Visual Design Studio without introducing a second asset or template model.

## Starter templates
- Corporate Employee ID Card — CR80 Front + Back
- Modern Business Card — 90×50 mm Front + Back
- Floral Invitation Card — A6 portrait
- Elegant Thank You Card — A6 landscape
- Botanical Product Label — 80×50 mm
- Premium Gift Voucher — 180×80 mm
- Simple Achievement Certificate — A4 landscape
- Minimal Packaging Front Panel — 100×140 mm

## Decorative vector assets
Ten embedded SVG assets: floral corners, botanical branch, divider, wreath, frame, mandala, vine, flower and ornamental corner.

Assets are stored through the existing `AssetReference` contract and placed as standard `SVG` design elements, so selection, move, resize, rotate, layers, grouping, copy/paste, undo/redo, alignment and snapping are inherited from the shared Design Engine.

No external network dependency is introduced.
